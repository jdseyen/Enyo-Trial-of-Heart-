**NARRATOR:** Just as she began to think it would walk away, it spoke softly.

**ENYO:** "Wait... all of them?!"

### PAGE 6 — ~30 words

**NARRATOR:** The creature nodded. Overjoyed, Enyo rushed to her mother with the good news.

**ENYO:** "Mama! Mama! You won't believe this!"

**NARRATOR:** While Enyo packed all the games carefully into a box, the creature asked in a gentle voice —

**CREATURE:** "What guides your kindness, young one?"

### PAGE 7 — ~18 words

**NARRATOR:** Enyo smiled brightly and replied,

**ENYO:** "My mama."

**CREATURE:** "Then... what do you wish for your future?"

**NARRATOR:** The question struck Enyo still.

### PAGE 8 — ~34 words

**NARRATOR:** Her mind offered nothing in return. It was simply blank — imagining a future beyond the shop felt uncertain, even unrealistic.

**ENYO:** "I'm not sure..."

**NARRATOR:** The creature fell silent for a moment.

**CREATURE:** "That's alright, young one... not all who wander are lost."

**NARRATOR:** Then it asked for a pen and a piece of paper.

### PAGE 9 — ~30 words
***The note.** Sacred line — typo-fix only. Trailing blank line removed.*

**NARRATOR:** Slowly, it wrote in silence, then passed the paper back to her.

**CREATURE** *(written)*:
> "You are worthy to seek the Cintamani Gem.
>
> Go, if you wish — let your kindness guide you; it has not led you astray."

### PAGE 10 — ~34 words

**NARRATOR:** And with that, the creature vanished, carrying the box of games with it.

**NARRATOR:** For the first time in a while, a spark of excitement returned to Enyo's mother.

**MOTHER:** "A Cintamani Gem! Enyo, do you realize how special this is?"

**ENYO:** "What is that, Mama?"

**MOTHER:** "Oh, sweetie... this legendary gem is given only to special ones like you."

### PAGE 11 — ~32 words
*P19 line protected — this is the confidence branch target.*

**MOTHER:** "You may wish for anything you desire — but only once."

**NARRATOR:** But to find it, she must face a test... one no one truly understands.

**NARRATOR:** Enyo wasn't as happy as her mother.

**ENYO:** "Anything...?" *Enyo's heart sank.*

**NARRATOR:** Surely a destiny like this belonged to someone greater than her.

### PAGE 12 — ~24 words
*Protected — high-confidence ending echo.*

**NARRATOR:** Enyo's mother noticed and drew her into a hug.

**MOTHER:** "Remember this, Enyo — no matter where life takes you... even if you doubt yourself, the world sees something in you."

### PAGE 13 — ~22 words
*Protected — the moral.*

**ENYO:** "What if... I can't do it?" *Enyo whispered.*

**NARRATOR:** Her mother gently held her hand.

**MOTHER:** "Opportunities do not find people who are perfect — they find people willing to grow."

### PAGE 14 — 17 words

**NARRATOR:** Enyo felt calm, trusting her mother's words.

**NARRATOR:** She made her decision — she would go, for her mama.

# ACT 4 — THE TRIAL

## Part One — The Mansion

### PAGE 1

**NARRATOR** *(chapter card)*: **ACT 4 — THE TRIAL BEGINS**

### PAGE 2

**NARRATOR:** Enyo arrived at a grand mansion, far grander than anything she had ever seen.

**ENYO:** "This... can't be right. Can it?"

**NARRATOR:** Suddenly, the gate creaked open on its own.

### PAGE 3

**NARRATOR:** As Enyo stepped into the mansion, she was greeted by a radiant figure cloaked in soft light.

**MADAME BLANCHE** *(warm, amused)*: "Welcome, Enyo. You may call me Madame Blanche. I've been waiting to finally meet you... in this form."

### PAGE 4

**NARRATOR:** Madame Blanche radiated a quiet elegance, warmth, and a strange sense of familiarity.

**ENYO:** "This might be rude, but... have we met before?"

### PAGE 5

**NARRATOR:** With a soft giggle, Madame Blanche gently patted Enyo's head.

**MADAME BLANCHE:** "Oh yes, we have, my dear... but first, let me take you to the trial room."

### PAGE 6

**NARRATOR:** Madame Blanche's expression softened, then grew serious.

**MADAME BLANCHE:** "You carry the purity qualified for the gem — but purity alone is not enough. Your knowledge and resilience will be tested."

### PAGE 7

**ENYO** *(muttering)*: "Oh no... knowledge? Resilience? ... What does that even mean?"

### PAGE 8

**NARRATOR:** Enyo's mind and heart began to race.

**ENYO:** "But... I'm not ready yet. This is too sudden!"

**NARRATOR:** Madame Blanche stopped in her tracks and turned to face Enyo.

### PAGE 9

**MADAME BLANCHE:** "Enyo, my dear... you'll never truly feel ready. You decide when you are."

**MADAME BLANCHE:** "Now... are you ready for this?"

### PAGE 10

**NARRATOR:** Enyo was reminded of her mother — waiting patiently for her back home.

**NARRATOR:** And so, with a deep breath, Enyo replied:

**ENYO:** "Yes... I am."

### PAGE 11

**NARRATOR:** A proud look crossed Madame Blanche's face as the door to the trial room opened.

**MADAME BLANCHE:** "Then let the trial... begin."

### PAGE 4

**NARRATOR:** She gave Madame Blanche nothing.

**NARRATOR:** Not because she didn't care —

### PAGE 5

**NARRATOR:** The guilt, embarrassment, and anger swallowed Enyo as a whole, leaving her too paralyzed to try again.

**NARRATOR:** Heavy.

**NARRATOR:** Inevitable.

### PAGE 11

**NARRATOR:** Yet somehow, her steps no longer felt as heavy as before.

### PAGE 12

**NARRATOR:** Perhaps her destiny was never meant to come from a wish.

**NARRATOR:** But from the courage to face life, even when she could not control its outcome.
